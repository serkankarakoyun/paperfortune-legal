Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

"Paper Fortune Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Paper Fortune YouTube channel to publish and manage the channel's own videos.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, the tool uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel, adds them to the channel's playlists, and reads and replies to comments on the channel's videos. It does not access any other user's account.

What it stores

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool does not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any third party. Public comments are read only to reply to them on YouTube.

Third parties

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
